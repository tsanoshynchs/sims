Idealizations in this model: point charges, Coulomb's law in vacuum (k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C²), source charges are treated as fixed in place (the test charge's own field is idealized away and never moves or perturbs them), purely electrostatic — nothing here evolves in time, so every value updates the instant you drag a charge or move a slider. Force-arrow lengths on the main scene are scaled to fit the display and are not on a fixed N-per-pixel scale; read the numeric labels for actual magnitude.
